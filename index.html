High-Performance Engineering

The Oyster Case

Crafted from Oystersteel—a superalloy designed for resistance to corrosion and extreme wear. Its polished lugs and side guards house screw-down chronograph pushers and a Triplock crown, ensuring absolute hermetic protection.

904L Oystersteel Alloy
40mm Case Diameter
Oyster Heritage in the Elements
Speed Measurement

Tachymetric Bezel

A key part of the Cosmograph Daytona's identity is its molded tachymetric scale bezel. It allows drivers to measure average speeds of up to 400 miles or kilometers per hour, perfectly aligning with motorsport performance.

400 Max Units/Hour
Cerachrom Monobloc Ceramic
Motorsport Heritage

Chronograph Dial

Equipped with sub-dials that function as elapsed time indicators. The central sweep second hand allows accurate reading of 1/8 of a second, while the two counters at 3 and 9 o'clock display the elapsed minutes and hours respectively.

1/8s Precision Sweep
30m & 12h Sub-Dial Counters
